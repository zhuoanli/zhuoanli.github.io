tldr: >-
  In 105 hypertrophic cardiomyopathy patients, those with HFpEF show reduced
  systolic and early-diastolic strain rates and greater diffuse and focal
  fibrosis on CMR; lower LV eGLSr, higher segmental ECV, atrial fibrillation
  and drinking independently predict HFpEF.
highlights:
  - '105 HCM patients at 3.0 T, classified as HFpEF (n = 46) or non-HF (n = 59) by the 2019 ESC HFA-PEFF algorithm'
  - 'HFpEF patients show reduced sGRSr, sGCSr, sGLSr and eGLSr, more LGE, and elevated native T1 and ECV'
  - 'Lower LV eGLSr, higher ECV in maximally hypertrophied segments, atrial fibrillation and drinking independently associate with HFpEF'
  - 'The H2FPEF score correlates with native T1, ECV and T2 in global and hypertrophied segments'
