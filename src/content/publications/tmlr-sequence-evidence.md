the accepted version: 2^5−1 input subsets enumerated over 9,393 complete
five-sequence studies and 35 findings; nested selection's per-finding gain
statistically indistinguishable from zero; prospectively specified
contrast–finding rankings validated on a second dataset of 1,351 brain-tumour
