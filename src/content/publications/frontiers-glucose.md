tldr: >-
  Multiparametric CMR in 108 hypertrophic cardiomyopathy patients shows that
  myocardial fibrosis and subclinical inflammation rise progressively with
  worsening glycemic status, and that ECV correlates strongly with HbA1c
  (r = 0.634), even in prediabetic patients.
highlights:
  - '108 HCM patients stratified by HbA1c into non-diabetic, prediabetic and diabetic subgroups, plus 30 healthy controls, all at 3.0 T'
  - 'Native T1 and ECV increase progressively from non-diabetic to prediabetic to diabetic HCM (p < 0.001)'
  - 'Mean ECV correlates with HbA1c at r = 0.634; the association holds in fully adjusted models'
  - 'Elevated T1 and T2 indicate subclinical myocardial inflammation in prediabetic and diabetic patients only'
